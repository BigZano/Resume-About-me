I work in IT in Washington. In my personal time I'm pursuing a Bachelor's degree from WGU, I'm the principal developer for Armory Bot, and I help moderate a large, thriving online community based around 40k, which is how sm2salamanders.site came to exist.

This site started as a boot.dev project and got out of hand. It's a dev diary, a workshop, and a place to put the things I'm into. I was enamored with the internet of the 2000s, and this is my attempt to reclaim a bit of that magic.
